# CurseForge page: Neutze's Macros
**Name:** Neutze's Macros
**Summary:** Pick your class spec and click a spell to get its macro on this character: one ability per macro, stance switch included.
**Categories:** Action Bars (main), Class
**License:** MIT · **Source:** https://github.com/neutze/wow-neutze-macros · **Logo:** art/logo-400.png

## Description
Open `/nm`, pick your spec and click a spell: its macro is created on this character (or updated) and put on your cursor. **Create / update all** fills the whole spec.

- One ability per macro. Stance or form switch first where needed (two presses in WoW Forever).
- Melee abilities start auto-attack. Caster spells don't, so wanding isn't interrupted.
- Mouseover where it helps.
- Presets for all 9 classes. Warrior uses the stance-dance set: focus Pummel, Death Wish/Recklessness with trinkets, Challenging Shout + Limited Invulnerability Potion, Intimidating Shout with stop-attack, auto Shoot/Throw.
